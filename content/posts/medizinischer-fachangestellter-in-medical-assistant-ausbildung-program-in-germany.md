The Medizinischer Fachangestellter/-in Ausbildung program is a vocational training program that prepares students to work in a medical setting, providing assistance to physicians and other healthcare professionals. This program typically lasts for three years and combines theoretical instruction in the classroom with practical training in the workplace.

During the program, students will learn about medical terminology, anatomy, physiology, medical billing, and patient care. They will also gain practical experience in a range of tasks, such as taking medical histories, performing diagnostic tests, and assisting with medical procedures.

In order to be eligible for the Medizinischer Fachangestellter/-in Ausbildung program, students must typically have completed their secondary education and possess a certain level of German language proficiency. Some programs may also require previous work experience or vocational training.

Once enrolled in the program, students will receive a salary from their employer, which will increase as they progress through the program. Upon completion of the program, students will receive a certificate that is recognized by German employers and can be used to demonstrate their qualifications and skills to potential employers.

The Medizinischer Fachangestellter/-in Ausbildung program provides students with a valuable opportunity to gain practical skills and knowledge in the medical field, while also earning a salary and receiving formal recognition for their qualifications. Upon completion of the program, students will be well-equipped to pursue a career as a medical assistant, or in related fields such as medical billing or medical administration. They may also have the opportunity to pursue further education and training in specialized areas of medicine.
