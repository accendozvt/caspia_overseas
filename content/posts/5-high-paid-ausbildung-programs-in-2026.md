# Beyond Nursing: 5 High-Paid Ausbildung Programs Accepting Foreigners in 2026

Date

January 2026

Target Audience

Students & Job Seekers in India (specifically Kerala)

Status

Active & High Demand

### Introduction: Think Outside the Hospital

When Malayalis think of Germany, they think of Nursing. It’s the standard advice: "Study Nursing, get a job, settle down."

But here is the truth for 2026: The Nursing sector is crowded. Admissions are getting competitive, and the work is physically demanding.

Meanwhile, Germany’s industrial heartland is screaming for talent in other sectors. These are jobs that pay as well as (or better than) nursing, have excellent work-life balance, and are actively recruiting from India under the Skilled Immigration Act 2026.

If you have an interest in machines, logistics, or technology, stop following the herd. Here are the Top 5 High-Paying Ausbildung Programs you probably haven't considered.

## Top 5 High-Paying Ausbildung Programs

#### 1. Mechatronics Technician

##### (Mechatroniker/in)

This is the "Royal Class" of technical Ausbildung. Germany is the land of cars and machines, and Mechatronics engineers keep them running. You are a hybrid of a mechanic and an electrician.

1st Year:

€1,050 – €1,150

2nd Year:

€1,150 – €1,250

3rd Year:

€1,250 – €1,350

Start Salary: €3,200 – €3,800/mo

Who is this for? Students who loved Physics and Maths in Plus Two. If you like fixing bikes or computers, this is for you.

Language Level: Strict B2 German.

#### 2. Chemical Technician

##### (Chemikant/in)

Germany has the largest chemical industry in Europe (think BASF, Bayer). You control the massive machines that produce chemicals, paints, and pharmaceuticals.

1st Year:

€1,100 – €1,200

2nd Year:

€1,200 – €1,300

3rd Year:

€1,300 – €1,450

Start Salary: €3,500 – €4,200/mo

Who is this for? Detail-oriented students with good Chemistry marks.

Bonus: The work is often less physically exhausting than nursing but requires high mental alertness.

#### 3. Railway in Operational Service

##### (Eisenbahner/in im Betriebsdienst)

Deutsche Bahn (DB) and private rail companies are desperate for staff. You can train to be a Train Driver (Lokführer) or a Dispatcher (Fahrdienstleiter).

1st Year:

€1,100 – €1,200

2nd Year:

€1,200 – €1,300

Start Salary: €3,000 – €3,600/mo

Who is this for? People who love travel, responsibility, and independent work.

Job Security: 100%. Trains will never stop running.

#### 4. Specialist in Warehouse Logistics

##### (Fachkraft für Lagerlogistik)

Germany is the export champion of the world. This is not just "moving boxes"—it’s high-tech management using advanced software (SAP).

1st Year:

€950 – €1,050

2nd Year:

€1,050 – €1,150

3rd Year:

€1,150 – €1,250

Start Salary: €2,500 – €3,000/mo

Who is this for? Good organizers who prefer active work over sitting at a desk all day.

Language Level: B1/B2 German.

#### 5. IT Specialist

##### (Fachinformatiker/in Systemintegration)

Did you know you can do IT without a 4-year B.Tech degree? You are the "Network Guy/Girl." You set up servers and solve hardware/software problems.

1st Year:

€1,050 – €1,150

2nd Year:

€1,150 – €1,250

3rd Year:

€1,250 – €1,400

Start Salary: €3,200 – €4,000/mo

Who is this for? Tech-savvy students who want to start earning at age 19 instead of waiting until 22.

Kerala Advantage: If you already have some computer knowledge, you will fly through this course.

### Why Choose These Over University?

- Zero Debt: No tuition fees. You get paid from Day 1.

- Job Guarantee: Companies train you because they need you. Retention rates are over 80%.

- Real Skills: You learn by doing, not just reading books.

- PR Pathway: Just like university graduates, Ausbildung graduates are skilled workers entitled to fast-track Permanent Residency.

### The "Catch" (Be Prepared)

- Language is Non-Negotiable: You need B2 German for all these roles. Machines and Chemicals don't forgive translation errors.

- Competition: While not as famous as nursing, German students want these jobs too. Your application (CV, Cover Letter) must be perfect.

## Final Verdict

If blood and hospitals aren't your thing, don't force yourself into Nursing. The German industry offers careers that are just as secure, lucrative, and respectable.
