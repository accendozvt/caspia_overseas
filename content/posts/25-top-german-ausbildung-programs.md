1. Bankkaufmann/-frau (Bank Clerk): This program teaches students about the banking system, financial services, and customer service skills.

2. Industriemechaniker/-in (Industrial Mechanic): This program focuses on mechanical engineering and teaches students how to design, construct, and maintain mechanical systems.

3. Elektroniker/-in (Electronics Technician): This program teaches students about electronic systems and how to design, install, and maintain them.

4. Kauffrau/-mann für Büromanagement (Office Management Clerk): This program teaches students about office management, communication, and administration.

5. Fachinformatiker/-in Anwendungsentwicklung (IT Specialist – Application Development): This program focuses on software development and programming.

6. Verkäufer/-in (Salesperson): This program teaches students about sales techniques, customer service, and product knowledge.

7. Zerspanungsmechaniker/-in (Machinist): This program teaches students about manufacturing processes, machine tool operations, and metalworking.

8. Medizinischer Fachangestellter/-in (Medical Assistant): This program teaches students about medical terminology, patient care, and medical record keeping.

9. Koch/Köchin (Cook): This program teaches students about culinary techniques, food preparation, and kitchen management.

10. Friseur/-in (Hairdresser): This program teaches students about hairstyling techniques, hair cutting, and salon management.

11. Florist/-in (Florist): This program teaches students about flower arrangements, plant care, and floral design.

12. Tischler/-in (Carpenter): This program teaches students about woodwork, furniture making, and construction techniques.

13. Metallbauer/-in (Metalworker): This program teaches students about metalworking, welding, and metal construction.

14. Maler/-in und Lackierer/-in (Painter and Decorator): This program teaches students about painting techniques, color theory, and decorating.

15. Augenoptiker/-in (Optician): This program teaches students about eyewear and optics, including lens crafting and frame fitting.

16. Fahrzeuglackierer/-in (Vehicle Painter): This program teaches students about automotive painting and refinishing techniques.

17. Orthopädieschuhmacher/-in (Orthopedic Shoe Maker): This program teaches students about orthopedic shoe design and construction, including custom orthotics.

18. Physiotherapeut/-in (Physiotherapist): This program teaches students about physical therapy techniques, anatomy, and rehabilitation.

19. Feinwerkmechaniker/-in (Precision Mechanic): This program teaches students about precision engineering, including designing and manufacturing precision components.

20. Veranstaltungskaufmann/-frau (Event Manager): This program teaches students about event planning, marketing, and management.

21. Chemielaborant/-in (Chemical Laboratory Technician): This program teaches students about chemical analysis, laboratory safety, and chemical research.

22. Kraftfahrzeugmechatroniker/-in (Automotive Mechatronics Technician): This program teaches students about automotive technology, including vehicle electronics, mechanics, and hydraulics.

23. Technischer Produktdesigner/-in (Technical Product Designer): This program teaches students about product design and development, including 3D modeling and prototyping.

24. Buchhändler/-in (Bookseller): This program teaches students about book selling, marketing, and bookkeeping.

25. Garten- und Landschaftsbauer/-in (Landscaper): This program teaches students about landscape design, construction, and maintenance.
